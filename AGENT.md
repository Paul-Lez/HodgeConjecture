This repo builds up a Lean formalisation of the Hodge Conjecture.

- Add definitions and abbreviations sparingly: each of these introduces extra indirection when reviewing the formalisation.
- Keep PR descriptions, comments, and issues *very* brief - at most a sentence or two is more than enough usually.
- Commit messages should be a single sentence.
- Documentation should be concise and to the point. Do not add any verbose justification or comparaison to other possible designs, or references to old parts of the codebase.
- Do not add any compatibility shims or layers unless explicitely instructed to.
- When writing in english (docs, or github), follow the ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English standard.
- Do not edit the README unless told to do so.
